## About Me

I'm a **Software Engineer** focused on building practical software, AI-powered tools, secure applications, and real-world digital products.

Through **DePrograma**, I document what I build, explain the engineering behind it, and share lessons from software development, AI, cybersecurity, APIs, backend systems, and SaaS.

My work spans:

- Full-stack web development
- Backend systems and APIs
- AI integrations and automation
- Mobile application development
- Secure application design
- SaaS and real-world product development

I primarily work with **JavaScript, Node.js, React, Next.js, Python, PHP, Flask, React Native, SQL, and modern web technologies**.

> I believe the best way to grow as an engineer is to build real systems, understand why they work, study why they fail, and share what you learn.
